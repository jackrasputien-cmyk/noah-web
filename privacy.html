N.O.A.H. Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026 · App: N.O.A.H. (id.noah.darurat) · Developer: independent developer, Indonesia · Also available in Bahasa Indonesia

N.O.A.H. is built to help in emergencies, with no account, no ads, and no developer-operated servers. The data the app uses is stored on your phone and is not sent to the developer.

Summary

  • The developer does not collect, store, or sell your personal data.
  • There are no analytics, trackers, or ads in the app.
  • Something is sent to another person only when you send it, for example an SMS with your location to your emergency contacts.

Data used and why

Data / permissionPurposeWhere it is stored
Location (GPS)Writing your location into the emergency SMS, recording your route, the way-back arrow, safe points, and automatic trail (only if you turn it on; off by default). While recording, a persistent "N.O.A.H. is recording your trail" notification is always shown.Only on your phone. Route history is kept for at least 60 days and can be deleted by you; the automatic trail keeps only the last ~6 hours.
ContactsChoosing emergency contacts from your address book.Only the contacts you choose, on your phone.
SMSSending an emergency message with your location to your emergency contacts, after you slide to confirm and the countdown ends. The app opens your phone's own SMS app with the message filled in; if permission to send SMS is granted in the future, the message may be sent directly after the same confirmation.Sent through your mobile carrier (standard SMS charges apply). N.O.A.H. does not read your SMS.
NotificationsReminders (e.g. resend location, end of trip) and the trail-recording indicator.—
Questions to the AI assistantAnswered by an AI model running on your phone, based on the guides stored in the app.Only on your phone. Never sent to the internet.

Internet and third parties

  • Core features do not need internet.
  • When you are online, the app may download an AI model and an Indonesian voice from public sources. These are file downloads only; no personal data is sent. A model can also be received from another phone via Quick Share, with your approval.
  • Text-to-speech uses the built-in Android service on your phone.
  • Voluntary donations via QRIS are handled by the payment app you choose, outside N.O.A.H.

Security and deletion

  • All data is kept in the app's storage on your phone.
  • You can delete route history, safe points, and emergency contacts at any time in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the phone.

Children

N.O.A.H. is not directed at children and does not ask for age. Children should be helped by a parent when setting up the app.

Limitations

N.O.A.H. is an aid, not a replacement for official emergency services or health workers. When possible, call 112, 119, or 115 (Indonesia).

Changes to this policy

The app is still in development. If a new feature changes how data is used (for example group features over Bluetooth), this policy will be updated on this page first.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue on GitHub (a contact email will be added)
